# 🎶 How do song events work in Ffans?

At **Ffans**, you don't just go to the stadium – you **make it vibrate**. Song events (chanting) are one of the most collective and exciting experiences you'll find in the app. Let us explain how they work and how to get the most out of them.

---

## 🎵 What is a song event?

Each song event already has a **song assigned** (for example, the club's anthem or a typical chant). The app **automatically downloads it** to your device. You don't have to choose or record anything – just be there and take part.

When the event starts, all the fans' devices that are participating **play the song in perfect sync**. The effect is magical: instead of sounding like individual noises, it sounds like **one single collective song**, much louder and more powerful. This reinforces the sense of togetherness and makes the stadium atmosphere spectacular.

---

## 🔊 How to take part correctly?

For the magic to work, there are **two key tips**:

1. **Turn the volume all the way up** 📱🔊  
   The higher the volume on each device, the stronger the unison effect. We make the song sound much louder and make everyone feel like they're part of one single voice.

2. **Sing along at the same time** 🗣️  
   The app already plays the song, but if you **sing along too**, the experience becomes even more vibrant. It's your real voice joining in with others, and the atmosphere becomes unstoppable.

---

## ✨ When you're away from home, it's even more special!

When the event is **away from home**, the stadium speakers play the home team's anthem. But you don't stay silent: from the app, **you can play your own team's anthem** and sing it loud to show your support on enemy turf. It's a unique way to say "we're here" and to cheer on your team wherever they are.

---

## 🏆 What about the scoring?

As we explained in the previous post, the action *"Joining in fan chants at a match"* has a **Total Score of 15 points** (10 from the category base + 5 from the specific event).

There are no multiple-choice answers or time adjustments: **if you take part, you get the full 15 points**. Simple and straightforward.
