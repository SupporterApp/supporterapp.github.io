# 🏅 How do Ffans badges work?

At **Ffans**, every match, every chant, and every answer shows your passion. But we want to go further: we want to recognise your journey and your commitment to your team. That's why we've created a **badge system** that evolves with you. Let us explain how they work, what unlocks them, and how you can reach the highest levels. ⚽🔥

---

## 🎯 1. What are badges?

Badges are **visual recognitions** that mark your milestones within the app. They don't depend on your total score, but are based on **three key areas** that measure your active participation:

|Category|What does it measure?|Icon|
|---|---|---|
|**Matches**|Number of matches you've attended|![Matches](/images/blog/fans.svg)|
|**Songs**|Number of songs you've sung during matches|![Songs](/images/blog/song.svg)|
|**Quizzes**|Number of football questions you've answered correctly|![Quizzes](/images/blog/question.svg)|

Each category has its own progress scale, and the badges you earn are displayed in the **"Badges" tab** on your progress page.

---

## 🥉🥈🥇💎 2. The 4 levels of each badge

For each category, we've defined **four levels** that increase in difficulty and prestige. As you surpass each threshold, your badge **changes colour, stars, and ornaments**, reflecting your growth as a fan.

|Level|Colour|Stars|Ornament|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Bronze** 🥉|Orange/copper|1|None|
|**Silver** 🥈|Bright grey|2|None|
|**Gold** 🥇|Golden|3|Wings|
|**Platinum** 💎|White/icy blue|4|Torch|

Every time you reach a new level, the badge updates automatically on your progress page. **You don't need to do anything to claim it!**

---

## 📈 3. What thresholds must I reach?

The thresholds are specific to each category and are designed to be progressive: the first levels are achievable, and the last ones require dedication. Here they are all in one table:

|Level|Matches|Songs|Quizzes|
|---|---|---|---|
|🥉 **Bronze**|1|1|1|
|🥈 **Silver**|5|3|5|
|🥇 **Gold**|15|10|15|
|💎 **Platinum**|30|25|40|

> **Example**: If you've attended 8 matches, your Matches badge will be **Silver level** (because you've surpassed 5 but haven't reached 15). When you reach 15, you'll automatically upgrade to **Gold**.

---

## 🎨 4. What do the badges look like?

Each badge has a unique design that combines:

- **Circular shape** with a metallic gradient for the corresponding level.
- **Central icon** that identifies the category:
  - ![Matches](/images/blog/fans.svg) **Matches**: the app's crest
  - ![Songs](/images/blog/song.svg) **Songs**: a musical note
  - ![Quizzes](/images/blog/question.svg) **Quizzes**: a question mark
- **Stars** at the bottom, which fill progressively (from left to right) as you level up.
- **Special ornaments** for the highest levels:
  - **Gold**: wings that extend above the badge.
  - **Platinum**: a torch that crowns the badge.

This visual design lets you recognise your progress in each area at a glance and motivates you to keep participating.

---

## 🔍 5. Where can I see my badges?

On the progress page (`Score / Level`), you'll find the **"Badges"** tab. There, the three categories are shown with the badge corresponding to your current level, along with the level name and progress toward the next one (e.g., *"Silver – 8/15"*).

Also, if you haven't yet unlocked the lowest level (Bronze), you'll see the badge in **grey** with 0 stars, indicating that you need to start participating in that category.

---

## 🌟 Fan tip

Badges not only give you recognition, but also show you which areas you can improve. If you see your Matches badge is Gold but your Songs badge is Bronze, it's time to sing louder at matches! 🎤🔥

And remember: **all categories count**. Being a complete fan isn't just about going to the field, but also living football with passion, voice, and knowledge.

---

## 🏁 In summary

- You have **3 badge categories**: Matches, Songs, and Quizzes.
- Each category has **4 levels**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.
- You advance automatically when you reach the defined thresholds for each level.
- Badges are shown with a progressive design that reflects your level.
- Check your progress in the **"Badges"** tab on the progress page.

So now you know: **participate, sing, answer, and level up**. Ffans badges are waiting for you! 💪🏅
