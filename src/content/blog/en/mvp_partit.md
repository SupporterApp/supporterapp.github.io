# 🏆 Match MVP: only the fans at the stadium decide it

At **Ffans** we believe football is much more than what you see on TV. Football is what you live in the stands, with a voice worn out from singing, with hands cold from clapping, with a heart racing in the final minutes. And if we're the ones living football, **we should also be the ones choosing the best player of the match**. ⚽🔥

---

## 🎯 Who decides the MVP?

**Only the fans who went to the stadium.** Period.

Not journalists, not TV commentators, not experts from a studio: the **Match MVP is voted exclusively by the community that was present at the stadium**, the ones who lived every pass, every shot and every gasp live. If you're there, you count. If you're not, you don't.

This makes the Ffans MVP **the only award truly given by the stands**. No middlemen, no interests, no opinions from people who haven't stepped on the grass.

---

## 🗳️ How does the voting work?

As the match nears its end, the app activates the MVP vote and it will appear as a **pending** event on the *My events* screen. The process is very simple:

1. **Enter the event**: when you access it, your attendance is registered automatically.
2. **You'll see the voting window**: with the start and end times. You must vote within this interval.
3. **Choose your player**: all the players of the match are shown. You just need to select one.
4. **Submit your vote**: once submitted, the vote is recorded.

---

## 🔄 What if I change my mind?

Here's one of the most interesting things: **while the voting window is open, you can change your vote as many times as you want**. If another player has just won you over, if you change your mind after discussing it with your fellow fans, or if you simply want to wait until the last moment, **you have the whole window to change it**.

When the window closes, the vote is locked and the counting begins.

---

## ⏳ What happens when the voting closes?

Once the window closes, **the event doesn't immediately move to attended**. First the system must:

1. **Collect all the votes** from the fans present.
2. **Calculate the final result** of the MVP.
3. **Record your participation** according to the result.

Only when the system finishes this whole process does the event appear in the **attended events** section with the corresponding score. Until then, you'll see it in the pending list.

> **Tip**: if you've voted but don't see the result yet, don't worry. The system is processing everyone's votes. It will appear within minutes or hours.

---

## 🏅 How many points do I get?

The MVP is part of the **Football Knowledge** category, and has a total score of **20 points**, broken down as follows:

|Concept|Points|
|---|---|
|Base score (Football Knowledge category)|5|
|Event score (guessing the MVP)|15|
|**Total Score**|**20**|

This score **is obtained when your vote matches the final MVP of the match**, that is, when you guess the player the community has chosen. It's not just about voting: it's about voting well. 🎯

> If you want to understand how categories and scoring work in general, we recommend the post **[Categories, events and scoring](/en/blog/categoria_event_puntacio/)**.

---

## 🌟 Why is the Ffans MVP special?

Because it's **the only MVP that represents you**. It's not a jury's award, nor a sports newsroom's, nor a TV channel's. It's the award that comes from the stands, from the people who sing, suffer and celebrate with you. It's the **community's** award.

And besides, it's another way to show that **you know football**. Because guessing the MVP isn't easy: you have to have followed the match closely, analyzed who was most decisive, and have footballing judgment. It's not enough to vote for the most famous player. 🧠⚽

---

## 🏁 In summary

- **Only those at the stadium** can vote for the MVP.
- The voting opens **at the end of the match** and has a **limited time window**.
- You can **change your vote** while the window is open.
- The result **isn't immediate**: the system calculates it when the event closes.
- **Guessing the MVP** gives you **20 points** (5 base + 15 event).
- It's **the only MVP decided by the stands**.

So now you know: at the next match, open Ffans, vote with your heart and your head, and show that your vision of football is as good as your passion. 💪🏆
