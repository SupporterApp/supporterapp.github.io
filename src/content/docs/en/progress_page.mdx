<h1>How do scoring and levels work?</h1>
<p>In the <strong>Score/Level</strong> section you can see all your activity as a fan. Every time you attend a match and complete the events that take place, you earn points that allow you to level up and unlock badges.</p>

<hr />

<h2>📊 How is your score calculated?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Match attendance:</strong> You will receive points for each match you attend. The score is counted when the match has started the second half and you open the app, or when you complete any event within the match.</li>
<li><strong>Support events (songs):</strong> To complete a support event, such as singing the team anthem, you must play the song for at least 20 seconds. This will give you bonus points.</li>
<li><strong>Knowledge events (questions):</strong> By correctly answering the match questions.</li>
<li><strong>Match MVP:</strong> The total score is <strong>20 points</strong>: 5 base points, as in the other knowledge events, and <strong>15 more if your vote matches the player most voted</strong> by the fans.</li>
</ul>

<h2>🏅 Levels and badges</h2>
<p>As you accumulate points, you will level up. Each level has a different name and icon. Additionally, you can earn special badges by reaching certain milestones (e.g., attending 10 matches, singing 20 songs, etc.). In the <em>Badges</em> tab you can see your progress towards each of them.</p>

<h2>📈 Ranking</h2>
<p>In the <em>Ranking</em> tab you can compare your score with other fans of your team. Only the top 50 per team are shown to encourage healthy competition.</p>

<p>Remember, the more you participate, the more points you earn and the higher you will climb in the ranking! Keep cheering for your team!</p>
