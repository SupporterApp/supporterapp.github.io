<h1>Welcome to Ffans!</h1>
<p>This app is made for you, the fans who make this sport great. We want to help you enjoy your team’s matches even more.</p>

<hr />

<h2>📝 Registration process</h2>
<p>To get started, you must:</p>
<ul>
<li>Choose your favorite team.</li>
<li>Pick an alias (name) that we will use to address you and that other fans will know you by.</li>
<li>Accept the terms of use and the privacy policy.</li>
</ul>

<h2>👤 Profile</h2>
<p>In this section you can view your registered data.</p>

<h2>🏆 Score and level</h2>
<p>Every time you attend a match and complete events, you earn points. As you accumulate points, you will level up and unlock badges. Check the Score/Level section to track your progress.</p>

<h2>📍 Go to the stadium</h2>
<p>When you are inside the stadium, tap this option. We will ask for location permission to confirm your presence. Once confirmed, you will be able to see all the match events and participate.</p>

<h2>📅 Events</h2>
<p>Alongside match events, there are also <strong>events that are not tied to a live match</strong>. You will find them in <em>My events</em> too, under pending, and you can complete them the same way: songs, questions and polls.</p>
<p>Remember that songs and questions can only be attended from the stadium, with the <em>Go to the stadium</em> option.</p>
