<h1>Pay attention to the events</h1>
<p>Once you have confirmed your attendance at the stadium, you will see the list of events that will appear during the match. Each event will give you the chance to participate and earn points.</p>

<hr />

<h2>🎵 Songs and support</h2>
<p>When a song is played (e.g., the team anthem), you must <span class="highlight">play it for at least 20 seconds</span> to complete the event. If you do, you will earn bonus points. The song will activate automatically at the scheduled time; stay tuned!</p>

<h2>❓ Knowledge questions</h2>
<p>You will be asked questions about the sport and the specific match. You must <span class="highlight">answer correctly</span> to complete the event.</p>

<h2>🕒 Time limit</h2>
<p>Some events have a start and end time (e.g., they can only be completed during a specific time window). Make sure to participate before the deadline.</p>

<h2>✅ Match attendance</h2>
<p>Match attendance will be automatically completed when:</p>
<ul>
<li>The second half has started and you open the app, or</li>
<li>You complete any of the match events.</li>
</ul>
